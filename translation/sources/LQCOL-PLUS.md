[h2] FAQ
[h3] Q1: Why is the LQCOL PLUS expansion tank essential for AI data center liquid cooling?
[p] A: As high-power AI servers surge past 100 kW/rack, severe temperature swings cause coolant to expand and contract. The LQCOL PLUS absorbs coolant expansion, prevents pressure spikes, eliminates pump cavitation, and safeguards quick disconnects and cold plates from leaks.
[h3] Q2: Is the tank compatible with 25% Propylene Glycol (PG25) or pure water?
[p] A: Yes. Our peroxide-cured EPDM diaphragm is specially formulated for 25% Propylene Glycol (PG25) and deionized water. It resists rubber swelling, hardening, and plasticizer leaching, ensuring long-term fluid purity and conductivity.
[h3] Q3: What is the difference between the All-Stainless Steel and Coated Carbon Steel models?
[p] A: The All-Stainless Steel (SUS304/316L) model is built for high-purity fluid loops with electropolished inner walls. The Carbon Steel model features a heavy-duty cold rolled carbon steel shell with a Triple-Grade Protective Coating for outstanding exterior corrosion resistance. Both feature a 304 SS connector.
[h3] Q4: Why is factory high-purity Nitrogen (N₂) pre-charge superior to compressed air?
[p] A: The factory 2 bar (30 psi) 99.9% dry nitrogen charge eliminates internal moisture and oxygen. This stops internal oxidation and prevents pressure drift caused by ambient temperature changes, enabling a 10+ year maintenance-free lifespan.
[h3] Q5: How does the expansion tank prevent pump cavitation in secondary loops?
[p] A: When coolant contracts due to sudden server load drops, a localized vacuum can occur. The tank provides continuous dynamic positive pressure, keeping pump inlet pressure stable to eliminate vapor bubble collapse, reducing pump noise and impeller wear.
[h3] Q6: Can the factory pre-charge pressure be adjusted for specific system requirements?
[p] A: Yes. While factory pre-charged to 2 bar (30 PSI) with high-purity nitrogen, the pre-charge pressure can be fine-tuned via the top air valve prior to installation to match your loop’s minimum operating pressure requirement.
[h3] Q7: How does the tank protect precision server cold plates from blockage or leakage?
[p] A: The electropolished stainless steel body or triple-coated shell prevents corrosion particles, while the EPDM diaphragm leaves zero chemical residues. This stops particle buildup in cold plate micro-channels and absorbs pressure spikes that cause QD leaks.

[h3] Q1: Why is the LQCOL PLUS expansion tank essential for AI data center liquid cooling?
[p] A: As high-power AI servers surge past 100 kW/rack, severe temperature swings cause coolant to expand and contract. The LQCOL PLUS absorbs coolant expansion, prevents pressure spikes, eliminates pump cavitation, and safeguards quick disconnects and cold plates from leaks.
[h3] Q2: Is the tank compatible with 25% Propylene Glycol (PG25) or pure water?
[p] A: Yes. Our peroxide-cured EPDM diaphragm is specially formulated for 25% Propylene Glycol (PG25) and deionized water. It resists rubber swelling, hardening, and plasticizer leaching, ensuring long-term fluid purity and conductivity.
[h3] Q3: What is the difference between the All-Stainless Steel and Coated Carbon Steel models?
[p] A: The All-Stainless Steel (SUS304/316L) model is built for high-purity fluid loops with electropolished inner walls. The Carbon Steel model features a heavy-duty cold rolled carbon steel shell with a Triple-Grade Protective Coating for outstanding exterior corrosion resistance. Both feature a 304 SS connector.
[h3] Q4: Why is factory high-purity Nitrogen (N₂) pre-charge superior to compressed air?
[p] A: The factory 2 bar (30 psi) 99.9% dry nitrogen charge eliminates internal moisture and oxygen. This stops internal oxidation and prevents pressure drift caused by ambient temperature changes, enabling a 10+ year maintenance-free lifespan.
[h3] Q5: How does the expansion tank prevent pump cavitation in secondary loops?
[p] A: When coolant contracts due to sudden server load drops, a localized vacuum can occur. The tank provides continuous dynamic positive pressure, keeping pump inlet pressure stable to eliminate vapor bubble collapse, reducing pump noise and impeller wear.
[h3] Q6: Can the factory pre-charge pressure be adjusted for specific system requirements?
[p] A: Yes. While factory pre-charged to 2 bar (30 PSI) with high-purity nitrogen, the pre-charge pressure can be fine-tuned via the top air valve prior to installation to match your loop’s minimum operating pressure requirement.
[h3] Q7: How does the tank protect precision server cold plates from blockage or leakage?
[p] A: The electropolished stainless steel body or triple-coated shell prevents corrosion particles, while the EPDM diaphragm leaves zero chemical residues. This stops particle buildup in cold plate micro-channels and absorbs pressure spikes that cause QD leaks.
